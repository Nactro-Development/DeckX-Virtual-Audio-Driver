# Privacy Policy for DeckX Virtual Audio Driver

**Effective Date:** September 11, 2026  
**Project:** DeckX Virtual Audio Driver  
**Maintainer:** Nactro Development (https://github.com/Nactro-Development)

---

## 1. Overview
Nactro Development is committed to respecting and protecting the privacy of all users. The **DeckX Virtual Audio Driver** is an open-source Windows virtual audio driver designed strictly for local audio playback and microphone loopback routing.

---

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect
- **No Personal Data:** We do not collect, process, or store names, email addresses, IP addresses, hardware identifiers, or any other personally identifiable information (PII).
- **No Audio Recording or Storage:** Audio routed through the virtual endpoints (DeckX Audio Cable and DeckX Virtual Microphone) is processed exclusively in transient system memory. It is never saved to disk, cached, or recorded by the driver.
- **No Telemetry or Tracking:** The driver contains no analytics libraries, tracking pixels, crash-reporting telemetry, or tracking mechanisms of any kind.
- **No Network Connectivity:** The driver operates entirely offline within the Windows kernel and user-mode audio stack. It makes no outgoing or incoming network requests.

---

## 3. Local Operation
All audio routing and digital signal transfers happen strictly within your local machine between user-selected applications (such as media players, games, Discord, or OBS Studio). 

---

## 4. Third-Party Access
Because no data is collected, no user information is ever sold, leased, rented, or shared with third parties or advertising services.

---
